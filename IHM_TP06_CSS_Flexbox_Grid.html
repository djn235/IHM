EX 01
Centring, three ways
Foundational15 min


A 300px-high banner must display a "Special offer" badge perfectly centred, horizontally and vertically. Produce three banners using three different techniques: (a) a flex container, (b) a grid container with a single declaration for alignment, (c) a flex container where the alignment is obtained by the badge itself through automatic margins. Explain in a comment why technique (c) works.




Why margin: auto centres in a flex containerIn flex layout, automatic margins absorb all the free space in their direction before justify-content and align-self apply. Auto margins on all four sides share the free space equally on both axes, centring the item.




EX 02
A responsive site footer
Foundational20 min


Build the footer of a bank's website: four columns (About, Products, Support, Contact) each containing a heading and a list of links, then a bottom bar with the copyright on the left and three legal links on the right. The columns must share the width equally but never become narrower than 180px, wrapping onto new lines when the space is insufficient, without any media query.




Key declarationflex: 1 1 180px: each column starts from a 180px basis, grows to share extra space and wraps when four bases no longer fit. The bottom bar uses justify-content: space-between and flex-wrap so that the legal links move under the copyright on small screens.

Part B: ApplicationExercises 3 and 4


EX 03
Media objects and an equal-height pricing row
Intermediate25 min


Part 1: build a list of three customer testimonials using the media object pattern: a 56px round avatar on the left that never shrinks, and on the right the name, company and a text of variable length.


Part 2: build a row of three pricing plans of equal height whatever the length of their feature lists. The central plan is highlighted (larger and with a "Most popular" ribbon), and the "Choose" button of every plan is aligned at the bottom of its card.






EX 04
A product photo gallery
Intermediate25 min


Create an image gallery for an online furniture shop with nine photographs (https://picsum.photos/id/N/600/600, ids 1 to 9 of your choice). Requirements:


  • as many columns as fit, each at least 160px wide, with 12px gaps;
  • all thumbnails square, images covering their cell without distortion;
  • the first photo is featured: it spans two columns and two rows;
  • the fifth photo spans two columns;
  • no hole is left in the grid, whatever the viewport width.




Filling the holesWith large items, the default auto-placement leaves gaps when an item does not fit at the end of a row. grid-auto-flow: dense back-fills them with later items. This alters the visual order, which is acceptable for a gallery of equivalent items but not for content whose order carries meaning.

Part C: MasteryExercises 5 and 6


EX 05
An application shell with named areas
Advanced30 min


Build the shell of a human-resources application: a header (logo, search field taking all remaining width, user avatar), a 240px navigation sidebar, a main area, a 280px notifications panel on the right and a footer. The layout must:


  • be defined by grid-template-areas;
  • fill at least the full viewport height, the footer staying at the bottom even when the content is short;
  • let the main area scroll independently when its content is long, while header and sidebar remain visible;
  • use Flexbox inside the header.




Independent scrollingThe shell is exactly 100vh high with grid-template-rows: auto 1fr auto; the main area gets overflow: auto and min-height: 0. The latter is essential: grid items default to min-height: auto, which would let the content stretch the row instead of scrolling.




EX 06
A Kanban board
Advanced35 min


Build a Kanban board for a marketing team with three columns: To do, In progress, Done. Each column has a header showing its title and a badge with the number of cards, aligned at opposite ends. Each card shows a title, a set of coloured tags that wrap onto several lines, and a footer with an avatar on the left and a due date on the right. Requirements:


  • the board is a grid of three columns of at least 240px; on narrow screens it scrolls horizontally rather than squashing the columns;
  • columns are flex containers stacking their cards with a 10px gap;
  • a card marked .urgent shows a red left border, and cards in the Done column are dimmed;
  • the "In progress" column may contain at most three cards: when it reaches that limit (the column carries a data-limit-reached attribute), its header turns orange.
