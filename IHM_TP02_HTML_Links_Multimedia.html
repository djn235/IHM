EX 01
Internal regulations with a table of contents
Foundational15 min


The internal regulations of a company have five articles: Working hours, Leave, Remuneration, Health and safety, Discipline. Create a single page in which:


  • a table of contents at the top links to each article through a fragment identifier;
  • each article is a section with an id in lowercase with hyphens (working-hours...);
  • each article ends with a "Back to contents" link;
  • the table of contents is a navigation landmark with an accessible name.




CheckClick the links in the preview: the address bar would show #leave, which also makes each article directly shareable and bookmarkable.




EX 02
A contact block with special links
Foundational15 min


Write the contact block of a micro-credit agency containing:


  • an e-mail link to credit@agence-tipaza.dz with the subject "Loan simulation" and a pre-filled body "Hello, I would like information about..." (spaces and punctuation correctly encoded);
  • a telephone link to the landline 024 47 12 34 in international format;
  • a link downloading docs/loan-conditions-2026.pdf under the name Loan_Conditions_2026.pdf, with its format and size announced in the text (PDF, 540 KB);
  • a link to the Bank of Algeria website opening in a new tab, secure and announced as such.




EncodingIn a URL, a space is encoded %20, a comma %2C and three dots stay as they are. Parameters of a mailto: URL are separated by &, written &amp; inside the HTML attribute.

EX 03
Path resolution drill
Intermediate20 min


Given the directory tree below, write the value of href/src for each case, then state what a browser would request if the path were written as root-relative instead (starting with /) and the site were published at https://user.github.io/erp/.




#FromTo


1index.htmlstyle.css


2modules/sales/orders.htmlstyle.css


3modules/sales/orders.htmlmodules/stock/items.html


4modules/stock/items.htmlmedia/products/p-101.webp


5modules/stock/items.htmlmodules/stock/reports/monthly.pdf


6modules/stock/reports/monthly.htmlindex.html#news




#Relative path


1assets/css/style.css


2../../assets/css/style.css


3../stock/items.html


4../../media/products/p-101.webp


5reports/monthly.pdf

The root-relative trap/assets/css/style.css resolves against the host, giving https://user.github.io/assets/css/style.css, outside the /erp/ project folder: a 404 error. Document-relative paths keep working wherever the site is deployed, which is why they are preferred for project sites.






EX 04
An accessible product gallery
Intermediate25 min


Create a gallery of four product photographs for a craft cooperative (pottery, carpet, basket, jewellery). Use the placeholder service https://picsum.photos/id/N/400/300 with ids 1080, 1059, 106 and 1062. Requirements:


  • each image in a figure with a caption giving the product name and price;
  • informative alternative text that does not duplicate the caption, intrinsic dimensions, lazy loading and asynchronous decoding;
  • each image is a link to its full-size version (/1200/900), and the link's accessible name states it opens the larger image;
  • a decorative separator image between the heading and the gallery, correctly ignored by assistive technologies.




Alt text and captionsWhen a caption already names the product, the alt describes what the picture shows (shape, colour, material), so a screen-reader user does not hear the same sentence twice. Because the image is the only content of the link, its alt also serves as the link text; adding "(enlarge)" states the link's function.

EX 05
A responsive hero banner
Advanced25 min


The marketing team provides one banner image in three crops: square 600 × 600 for phones, landscape 1200 × 500 for tablets, panorama 1920 × 600 for desktops, each available in AVIF, WebP and JPEG. Write a picture element that:


  1. serves the square crop up to 599px, the landscape crop from 600px to 1199px and the panorama from 1200px;
  2. offers, for each crop, AVIF then WebP with JPEG as fallback;
  3. is not lazily loaded (it is above the fold) but is fetched with high priority;
  4. has an alternative text and dimensions.


How many source elements are required? Justify.




Answer: eight sourcesThree crops × two modern formats = 6 sources for AVIF and WebP, plus 2 JPEG sources for the square and landscape crops; the panorama JPEG is carried by the final img. The browser stops at the first source whose media and type both match, so within each crop the formats are listed from the most to the least efficient. A hero image is the Largest Contentful Paint candidate: lazy loading would delay it, while fetchpriority="high" brings it forward.




EX 06
An accessible and secure media page
Advanced30 min


Build the "Discover our cooperative" page combining:


  • a presentation video (MDN sample flower.webm / flower.mp4) with poster, controls, metadata preloading, inline playback, English captions by default, French subtitles and a fallback link;
  • a podcast episode in audio with a text transcript revealed by a details/summary disclosure;
  • an OpenStreetMap frame of Cherchell, named, lazy, sandboxed with the minimum privileges needed to display the map, and with no referrer;
  • under the map, a text alternative giving the postal address and a link to a larger map.




Accessibility rationaleCaptions serve deaf and hard-of-hearing users (WCAG 1.2.2); a transcript serves audio content (WCAG 1.2.1); the address under the map ensures that the information is not available only through a visual, mouse-driven widget.
