EX 01
A secure login form
Foundational15 min


Create the login form of the faculty's digital platform: an identifier (the student's university e-mail), a password, a "Remember me" checkbox, a submit button and a link "Forgotten password?". The form must:


  • use the correct HTTP method and send the data to /auth/login;
  • allow password managers to autofill the fields (correct autocomplete tokens);
  • require both fields, with a minimum password length of 8;
  • give every control a visible label.




Why current-password?The token current-password tells password managers to propose the stored password, whereas new-password (registration) tells them to generate one. Using username on the identifier pairs the two fields.




EX 02
A student satisfaction survey
Foundational20 min


Build a survey about the faculty cafeteria, sent by GET to /survey:


  • Overall satisfaction from 1 to 5 as a group of radio buttons with a legend (one answer required).
  • Services used (several answers): breakfast, lunch, dinner, take-away.
  • Waiting time estimated with a slider from 0 to 30 minutes in steps of 5, its current value displayed in an output.
  • Free comments limited to 300 characters.


Submit the form in the preview and examine the resulting query string: what is sent for the checkboxes that are not ticked?




AnswerNothing: an unchecked checkbox is not a successful control. Ticked boxes sharing the same name are sent as repeated pairs (services=lunch&services=dinner). The small inline script only mirrors the slider value; the form works without it.

Part B: ApplicationExercises 3 and 4


EX 03
A job application with file upload
Intermediate25 min


Create the application form for an internship at a bank. Fields: full name, e-mail, mobile phone, desired branch (select with two groups: Tipaza: Kolea, Cherchell; Algiers: Hydra, Bab Ezzouar), start date (not before 1 February 2027), a CV (PDF only, required), up to three supporting documents (PDF or image, multiple), a motivation letter (200 to 2 000 characters) and consent to data processing.


Which method and enctype are mandatory here, and why?




AnswerFiles cannot be transmitted as URL-encoded text: the form must use method="post" and enctype="multipart/form-data", which splits the body into parts, one per field, each with its own content type. The accept attribute only filters the file picker; the server must still verify the actual file type.

EX 05
A hotel booking form
Advanced30 min


Design the booking form of a seaside hotel in Tipaza, in three fieldsets:


  • Stay: arrival and departure dates (no earlier than 1 November 2026), number of adults (1 to 4) and of children (0 to 3), room type as radio buttons (Single, Double, Family suite), board (select: room only, breakfast, half board).
  • Guest: civility, last name, first name, e-mail, phone, country with a datalist of suggestions (Algeria, Tunisia, Morocco, France), all with correct autocomplete tokens.
  • Options: sea view, airport transfer, late check-out (checkboxes), special requests (textarea, 500 characters).


Every required field must be indicated in text, and help texts must be programmatically associated.




A limit of native validationHTML alone cannot express that the departure must follow the arrival: such cross-field rules require JavaScript (setCustomValidity, Session 11) and, in any case, server-side validation.
