Part A: FoundationsExercises 1 and 2


EX 01
A set of button micro-interactions
Foundational20 min


Create three button styles for an e-banking interface, each with feedback in 150 to 250ms:


  • Primary ("Transfer"): rises by 2px with a deeper shadow on hover and keyboard focus; returns instantly on press.
  • Secondary ("Cancel"): outlined; on hover the background fills from left to right (hint: a gradient background whose background-size transitions).
  • Icon button ("Refresh", a circular arrow drawn as a character or border): the icon rotates a half-turn on hover.


All buttons must keep a visible focus ring, and only the properties that change may be listed in transition.






EX 02
Navigation underline and dropdown
Intermediate25 min


Build a navigation bar of four items in which:


  1. an underline grows from the centre of the link outwards on hover and focus (use ::after and scale);
  2. the item "Services" opens a dropdown submenu on hover and when any element inside it receives keyboard focus (:focus-within);
  3. the submenu fades in while sliding down by 8px in 200ms; when hidden it must not be reachable or clickable.




Hiding without breaking the transitiondisplay: none cannot be transitioned. The submenu is therefore hidden with opacity: 0, visibility: hidden and pointer-events: none; visibility is interpolated discretely, so it switches at the right moment of the transition and removes the links from the tab order while hidden.

Part B: ApplicationExercises 3 and 4


EX 03
Loading indicators
Intermediate25 min


Create three loading indicators for a reporting dashboard:


  • a dual-ring spinner: two concentric rings rotating in opposite directions at different speeds;
  • an indeterminate progress bar: a segment sliding endlessly from left to right inside a track;
  • a determinate progress bar whose filled width is controlled by a custom property --value (0 to 100) set inline, and which animates smoothly when the value changes. Provide two buttons to change the value for testing.


Each indicator must expose its meaning to assistive technologies (role="status" or role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow).




Why transform: scaleX?The determinate bar animates transform: scaleX(calc(var(--value) / 100)) with transform-origin: left rather than width, so each frame is produced by the compositor without recomputing layout.




EX 04
An interactive product card
Intermediate25 min


Create a product card for an online electronics shop. On hover or focus within the card:


  • the image zooms in slightly inside its frame (the frame does not grow);
  • a dark overlay slides up from the bottom, revealing a "Quick view" button;
  • the whole card tilts slightly in 3D (a few degrees around the X and Y axes) with perspective.


The card must remain fully usable with the keyboard, and the tilt must be removed under reduced motion while the overlay still appears (with a simple fade).

EX 05
Self-dismissing notifications
Advanced30 min


Using a single @keyframes rule, create a notification ("toast") that slides in from the bottom, stays visible, then slides out, over a total duration of 4 seconds, with no JavaScript for the timing. Then stack three toasts (success, warning, error) that appear one after another with a 600ms offset.


  1. At which percentages must the keyframes be placed so that the entry and exit each last 400ms?
  2. Which fill mode is needed so that the delayed toasts are invisible before their turn and remain hidden at the end?
  3. When does the third toast disappear completely?




Answers(1) 400ms / 4 000ms = 10%: keyframes at 0%, 10%, 90% and 100%. (2) both: backwards applies the 0% state during the delay, forwards keeps the 100% state afterwards. (3) The third toast starts at 2 × 600 = 1 200ms and ends at 1 200 + 4 000 = 5 200ms.




AccessibilityToasts disappear by themselves, which can prevent some users from reading them (WCAG 2.2.1). Here they pause on hover and focus, and essential messages (errors) should in practice remain until dismissed. The container is a live region so that screen readers announce each message.
